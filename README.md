# Education for All: Donor & Donation Analysis

A SQL-driven analysis of the Education for All charity's donor and donation data, built as part of a 10Alytics internship project. Identifies high-value donor segments (by state, job field, car, donation frequency) and a key finding: donors who speak a second language and have lower education levels give less and less often, pointing to a language-barrier problem in the charity's email outreach.

## Contents

- `Education_For_All_Donor_Insights_Report.docx` — full write-up: methodology, root cause analysis, SQL queries, insights, and recommendations.

## Key findings

- Total donations: $249,085 across 1,000 donors (48% from female donors, 52% from male donors).
- Donation value and frequency correlate strongly with second-language ability and education level.
- Top-donating states: California, Texas, Florida, New York, Virginia.
- Top-donating job field: Human Resources ($23,060); lowest: Legal ($17,309).

## Recommendation

Offer translated correspondence for donors who prefer a second language, increase outreach in low-donation states, and build loyalty programs for recurring donors.
